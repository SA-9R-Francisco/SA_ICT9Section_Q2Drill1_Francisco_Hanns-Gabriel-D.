A Guide to Brazillian Jiu-Jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) is a martial art and combat sport that focuses on grappling and ground fighting. It stems from Judo which mainly focuses on stand-up fighting.



Basic Needs
  • Gi
  • Rash Guard
  • Shorts
  • Belt
  • Water Bottle
  • Towels
Basic Respect
  • Bow before entering the mat
  • Always maintain cleanliness
  • Always be kind to your partners
  • Make your goal not to hurt anyone
Basic Techniques
  • Osoto Gari(A large outer reap on the leg)
  • Seoi Nage(The basic judo throw)
  • O-goshi(The hip throw)
  • Arm Bar(The basic on-ground submission)
  • Side Control(To control your opponent with many different openings for moves)
